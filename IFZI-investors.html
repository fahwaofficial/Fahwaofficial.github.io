01 / Project overview

What we are building

IFZI (formerly known as IFC / IFCoin) is a community-focused Jetton on TON. The project combines an on-chain token and decentralized-market access with a planned 24-hour mining/claim cycle, task-based participation and referral features.

The product is being developed as a lightweight web experience with TON wallet connectivity. The current priority is to make wallet proof, mining claims, reward accounting and withdrawals reliable, auditable and easy for users to understand.

Development status: The mining portal is in active development and technical validation. We do not present mining, withdrawals, user traction, audits or LP locking as completed unless independently verifiable evidence is published. No token price appreciation or investment return is promised.

Why TON

  • Native access to Telegram communities and TON wallets.
  • Low-friction on-chain transfers and public transaction records.
  • Opportunity to build an accessible community product around a TON Jetton.
