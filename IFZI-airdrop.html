CAMPAIGN RULES

How the IFZI airdrop works

  • Connect a TON Mainnet wallet using TON Connect.
  • Complete Telegram, YouTube and X tasks.
  • Submit one accessible proof link for each task.
  • The IFZI team manually checks every submission.
  • Each approved task credits 1,000 IFZI to your website balance.
  • All three approvals are required before you can request a withdrawal.
  • Withdrawal requests are manually reviewed and paid by the project team.

This campaign is a community rewards program, not proof-of-work mining, investment advice or a promise of future token value. Availability is subject to campaign rules and the remaining reward allocation.
