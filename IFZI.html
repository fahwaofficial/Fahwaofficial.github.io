FAQ

Common Questions

Does mining continue if I close the website?

Yes. The server stores the start and end timestamps. When you return, it shows the remaining time or the completed claim.

Are points real IFZI tokens immediately?

No. Points are internal reward credits in the project's database. Actual IFZI tokens are sent only after a withdrawal request is reviewed and manually paid from the treasury.

Can I increase my level?

Yes. The server checks your on-chain IFZI wallet balance when a new 24-hour cycle starts. The level for that cycle remains fixed until it ends.

Is the 10% referral reward additional?

No. It is shared from the invitee's gross mining reward, so total pool emissions do not increase.

Does this require changing the IFZI token contract?

No. Mining points and referral accounting are off-chain. The existing Jetton contract is not modified.
