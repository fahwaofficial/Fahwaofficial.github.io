Who is Fahwa?

Fahwa is an independent Persian Trap artist from Karaj, Iran. He officially began his music career in 2020 with the goal of creating a unique sound that blends emotion, storytelling, and modern trap production.

Since starting his journey, Fahwa has independently released six official tracks across major streaming platforms. His music reflects personal experiences, ambition, and the pursuit of artistic identity.

Musical Style

Genre: Trap
Language: Persian
Based in: Karaj, Iran

Career

Since 2020, Fahwa has been consistently developing his artistic vision and expanding his presence across digital music platforms. Every release represents a new chapter in his evolution as an independent artist.

Vision

Fahwa aims to build a lasting artistic identity through authentic music, high-quality production, and continuous creative growth. His long-term vision is to reach international audiences while representing Persian trap music on a global stage.
